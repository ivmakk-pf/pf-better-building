## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added

- Building over tree stumps, rocks, props, and the ruins of the map. Your own constructions, players, and animals still block a placement.
- Building in no-build zones. "EFFECT RESTRICTED" still blocks a placement.
- Move mode: press `V` with the Building Tool in build mode, click a highlighted workstation, storage box, piece of furniture, lamp, or decoration, and place it at a new spot. The construction keeps its items, durability, fuel, and workstation upgrades, and a lit brazier takes its heat with it.
- A blue highlight on the construction that you look at in Move mode, and a "Move construction" line in the hint list.
- `config.lua` with a switch for each feature and the Move mode key.
